Source of these MP3 renderings: https://tonejs.github.io/audio/salamander/
Only the 5 anchors needed for C4-B4 are kept; the app resamples each by at
most one semitone to reach the notes in between.

Modifications made for this app -- trimmed from ~16s to 2.0s, faded out from
1.4s, re-encoded to 128 kbps MP3 / 44.1 kHz:

  ffmpeg -i ORIGINAL.mp3 -t 2.0 -af "afade=t=out:st=1.4:d=0.6" \
         -c:a libmp3lame -b:a 128k -ar 44100 OUT.mp3

To change the note length, re-run that with a new -t (and move the fade
start to 0.6s before it). Originals can be re-fetched from the URL above.
