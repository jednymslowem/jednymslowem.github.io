Chores that pay,
on the Apple Watch.

Mom Bux turns everyday chores into a reward currency your kids earn — and spend — right from their wrist. Parents keep the books on their phone.

Early access See how it works Coming to the App Store

Requires an Apple Watch. Data stays in your own iCloud.

01

Earn from the wrist

Kids open Mom Bux on their watch, tap Earn, and pick a chore. No phone required — the watch is the whole experience.

Watch — Earn / Spend
02

Start the timer, get paid

Pick a chore and start a countdown. When it finishes, the Mom Bux land in the balance automatically. In a hurry? Long-press to log it instantly.

Watch — countdown timer
03

Spend what they earn

Tap Spend to cash Mom Bux in for whatever your family agrees on — screen time, treats, a later bedtime.

Watch — Spend tiles
04

Parents keep the books

A simple, view-only hub on iPhone shows every kid and their current balance. Pair in seconds by scanning the QR the watch shows.

iPhone — parent dashboard

Private by design

Your family's data stays yours.

No accounts, no ads, no analytics, no third-party tracking. Everything syncs through your own iCloud, and each kid's ledger is encrypted on the device before it ever leaves — the keys never touch the cloud.
